Lagrange's Theorem, one of the most important results in finite group theory, states that
the order of a subgroup must divide the order of the group. This theorem provides a powerful
tool for analyzing finite groups; it gives us an idea of exactly what type of subgroups we
might expect a finite group to possess. Central to understanding Lagrange's Theorem is the
notion of a coset.

> *A coset is not a set here: `2b4m` has no sort of subsets of a group, so $gH$ is
> represented by its membership predicate `inCoset(g, x)` — "$x \in gH$" — and the set
> equality $g_1H = g_2H$ becomes extensional agreement of two predicates.*

## Definition

**Definition.** Let $G$ be a group and $H$ a subgroup of $G$. Define a **left
coset** of $H$ with **representative** $g \in G$ to be the set
$$gH = \{ gh : h \in H \}.$$
**Right cosets** can be defined similarly by $Hg = \{ hg : h \in H \}$. If left and
right cosets coincide or if it is clear from the context to which type of coset we
are referring, we will use the word *coset* without specifying left or right.

We write $H$ for the subgroup-membership relation, so that `inH(h)` is "$h \in H$",
and `inCoset(g, x)` for "$x \in gH$".

We will always write the cosets of subgroups of ${\mathbb Z}$ and ${\mathbb Z}_n$ with the
additive notation we have used for cosets here. In a commutative group, left and right
cosets are always identical. It is not always the case that a left coset is the same as a
right coset.

> *Judson illustrates all of this with worked cosets in $\mathbb{Z}_6$ and $S_3$ — numeric
> walkthroughs, not transliterated here. In $S_3$ the subgroup
> $H = \{(1), (1\,2\,3), (1\,3\,2)\}$ has its left and right cosets coinciding, while
> $K = \{(1), (1\,2)\}$ does not.*

Every element lies in its own coset — the fact that makes the cosets *cover* $G$.

> NB: This theorem exists in std in `std/group/coset.b4m:58:elementIsInItsOwnCoset`.

```2b4m
theorem elementIsInItsOwnCoset: forall g: G; inCoset(g, g)
proof
  @generalize-g |
    fix g: G {
      @identity-is-in |
        inH(e)
        [by cite subgroupHasIdentity]
      @g-times-identity |
        op(g, e) = g
        [using specialize grpIdentityRight(g)]
      @g-is-g-times-identity |
        g = op(g, e)
        [by symmetry g-times-identity]
      @witnessed |
        inH(e) and g = op(g, e)
        [by both identity-is-in g-is-g-times-identity]
      @conclusion-in-its-coset |
        inCoset(g, g)
        [by witness(e) witnessed]
    }
  @conclusion |
    forall g: G; inCoset(g, g)
    [by generalize generalize-g]
qed
```

## Lemma 6.3 — the equivalence

**Lemma 6.3.** Let $H$ be a subgroup of a group $G$ and suppose that
$g_1, g_2 \in G$. The following conditions are equivalent.

1. $g_1 H = g_2 H$;
2. $H g_1^{-1} = H g_2^{-1}$;
3. $g_1 H \subset g_2 H$;
4. $g_2 \in g_1 H$;
5. $g_1^{-1} g_2 \in H$.

Judson leaves the proof as an exercise. The hinge of the cycle is the equivalence of
(4) and (5) — "$g_2$ lies in $g_1H$" iff "$g_1^{-1}g_2$ lies in $H$" — and it is the
form every later argument uses.

```2b4m
theorem membershipIffQuotientIsInH: forall g: G; forall x: G;
  inCoset(g, x) iff inH(op(inv(g), x))
proof
  @generalize-g |
    fix g: G {
      @generalize-x |
        fix x: G {
          @given-in-coset |
            assume inCoset(g, x) {
              @in-coset |
                inCoset(g, x)
                [by hypothesis given-in-coset]
              @witness-exists |
                exists h: G; inH(h) and x = op(g, h)
                [using tautology in-coset]
              @with-witness |
                unpack h: G from witness-exists {
                  @h-facts |
                    inH(h) and x = op(g, h)
                    [by hypothesis with-witness]
                  @h-in |
                    inH(h)
                    [by and_lhs h-facts]
                  @x-is-g-h |
                    x = op(g, h)
                    [by and_rhs h-facts]
                  // g⁻¹x = g⁻¹(gh) = (g⁻¹g)h = eh = h
                  @quotient-at-x |
                    op(inv(g), x) = op(inv(g), op(g, h))
                    [by rewrite x-is-g-h quotient-reflexive]
                  @quotient-reflexive |
                    op(inv(g), x) = op(inv(g), x)
                    [by reflexivity]
                  @regrouped |
                    op(inv(g), op(g, h)) = op(op(inv(g), g), h)
                    [using assoc(grpAssoc)]
                  @inv-cancels |
                    op(inv(g), g) = e
                    [using specialize grpInverseLeft(g)]
                  @with-identity |
                    op(op(inv(g), g), h) = op(e, h)
                    [by rewrite inv-cancels regrouped-reflexive]
                  @regrouped-reflexive |
                    op(op(inv(g), g), h) = op(op(inv(g), g), h)
                    [by reflexivity]
                  @identity-times-h |
                    op(e, h) = h
                    [using specialize grpIdentityLeft(h)]
                  @quotient-is-h |
                    op(inv(g), x) = h
                    [using chain quotient-at-x regrouped with-identity identity-times-h]
                  @conclusion-quotient-in |
                    inH(op(inv(g), x))
                    [by rewrite quotient-is-h h-in]
                }
              @conclusion-forward |
                inH(op(inv(g), x))
                [by unpacked with-witness]
            }
          @forward |
            inCoset(g, x) -> inH(op(inv(g), x))
            [by discharge given-in-coset]
          @given-quotient-in |
            assume inH(op(inv(g), x)) {
              @quotient-in |
                inH(op(inv(g), x))
                [by hypothesis given-quotient-in]
              // x = g(g⁻¹x), and g⁻¹x is in H
              @regrouped-back |
                op(g, op(inv(g), x)) = op(op(g, inv(g)), x)
                [using assoc(grpAssoc)]
              @inv-cancels-right |
                op(g, inv(g)) = e
                [using specialize grpInverseRight(g)]
              @with-identity-back |
                op(op(g, inv(g)), x) = op(e, x)
                [by rewrite inv-cancels-right regrouped-back-reflexive]
              @regrouped-back-reflexive |
                op(op(g, inv(g)), x) = op(op(g, inv(g)), x)
                [by reflexivity]
              @identity-times-x |
                op(e, x) = x
                [using specialize grpIdentityLeft(x)]
              @product-is-x |
                op(g, op(inv(g), x)) = x
                [using chain regrouped-back with-identity-back identity-times-x]
              @x-is-the-product |
                x = op(g, op(inv(g), x))
                [by symmetry product-is-x]
              @witnessed-back |
                inH(op(inv(g), x)) and x = op(g, op(inv(g), x))
                [by both quotient-in x-is-the-product]
              @conclusion-backward |
                inCoset(g, x)
                [by witness(op(inv(g), x)) witnessed-back]
            }
          @backward |
            inH(op(inv(g), x)) -> inCoset(g, x)
            [by discharge given-quotient-in]
          @conclusion-iff |
            inCoset(g, x) iff inH(op(inv(g), x))
            [by make_equivalence forward backward]
        }
      @discharge-x |
        forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) iff inH(op(inv(g), x))
        [by generalize generalize-x]
    }
  @conclusion |
    forall g: G; forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) iff inH(op(inv(g), x))
    [by generalize generalize-g]
qed
```

Conditions (1) and (4) are equivalent too: two cosets agree exactly when each
representative lies in the other's coset.

```2b4m
theorem cosetsCoincideIffQuotientIsInH: forall g: G; forall k: G;
  (forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) iff inCoset(k, x)) iff inH(op(inv(g), k))
proof
  @generalize-g |
    fix g: G {
      @generalize-k |
        fix k: G {
          @given-same |
            assume forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) iff inCoset(k, x) {
              @same |
                forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) iff inCoset(k, x)
                [by hypothesis given-same]
              @k-in-its-own |
                inCoset(k, k)
                [using specialize elementIsInItsOwnCoset(k)]
              @same-at-k |
                inCoset(g, k) iff inCoset(k, k)
                [using specialize same(k)]
              @k-in-g-coset |
                inCoset(g, k)
                [using tautology same-at-k k-in-its-own]
              @criterion-at-k |
                inCoset(g, k) iff inH(op(inv(g), k))
                [using specialize inCosetIffQuotientIsInTheSubgroup(g, k)]
              @conclusion-forward |
                inH(op(inv(g), k))
                [using tautology criterion-at-k k-in-g-coset]
            }
          @forward |
            (forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) iff inCoset(k, x)) -> inH(op(inv(g), k))
            [by discharge given-same]
          @given-quotient |
            assume inH(op(inv(g), k)) {
              @quotient-in |
                inH(op(inv(g), k))
                [by hypothesis given-quotient]
              // the reverse quotient k⁻¹g = (g⁻¹k)⁻¹ is in H too.
              @reverse-is-the-inv |
                op(inv(k), g) = inv(op(inv(g), k))
                [using chain reverse-via-invProduct inv-of-inv-inside]
              @reverse-via-invProduct |
                op(inv(k), g) = op(inv(k), inv(inv(g)))
                [by rewrite inv-of-g-twice reverse-reflexive]
              @reverse-reflexive |
                op(inv(k), g) = op(inv(k), g)
                [by reflexivity]
              @inv-of-g-twice |
                inv(inv(g)) = g
                [using specialize invInvolution(g)]
              @inv-of-inside |
                inv(op(inv(g), k)) = op(inv(k), inv(inv(g)))
                [using specialize invProduct(inv(g), k)]
              @inv-of-inv-inside |
                op(inv(k), inv(inv(g))) = inv(op(inv(g), k))
                [by symmetry inv-of-inside]
              @reverse-quotient-in |
                inH(op(inv(k), g))
                [by rewrite reverse-is-the-inv reverse-as-inv-in]
              @reverse-as-inv-in |
                inH(inv(op(inv(g), k)))
                [using specialize subgroupClosedInverse(op(inv(g), k)) quotient-in]
              @generalize-x |
                fix x: G {
                  @criterion-g |
                    inCoset(g, x) iff inH(op(inv(g), x))
                    [using specialize inCosetIffQuotientIsInTheSubgroup(g, x)]
                  @criterion-k |
                    inCoset(k, x) iff inH(op(inv(k), x))
                    [using specialize inCosetIffQuotientIsInTheSubgroup(k, x)]
                  // k⁻¹x = (k⁻¹g)(g⁻¹x) and g⁻¹x = (g⁻¹k)(k⁻¹x) — each quotient is the other
                  // times a subgroup element, so each membership follows from the other.
                  @k-quotient-splits |
                    op(inv(k), x) = op(op(inv(k), g), op(inv(g), x))
                    [using specialize quotientSplits(k, g, x)]
                  @g-quotient-splits |
                    op(inv(g), x) = op(op(inv(g), k), op(inv(k), x))
                    [using specialize quotientSplits(g, k, x)]
                  @given-in-g |
                    assume inH(op(inv(g), x)) {
                      @in-g |
                        inH(op(inv(g), x))
                        [by hypothesis given-in-g]
                      @product-in |
                        inH(op(op(inv(k), g), op(inv(g), x)))
                        [using specialize subgroupClosedOp(op(inv(k), g), op(inv(g), x)) reverse-quotient-in in-g]
                      @conclusion-in-k |
                        inH(op(inv(k), x))
                        [by rewrite k-quotient-splits product-in]
                    }
                  @g-implies-k |
                    inH(op(inv(g), x)) -> inH(op(inv(k), x))
                    [by discharge given-in-g]
                  @given-in-k |
                    assume inH(op(inv(k), x)) {
                      @in-k |
                        inH(op(inv(k), x))
                        [by hypothesis given-in-k]
                      @product-in-back |
                        inH(op(op(inv(g), k), op(inv(k), x)))
                        [using specialize subgroupClosedOp(op(inv(g), k), op(inv(k), x)) quotient-in in-k]
                      @conclusion-in-g |
                        inH(op(inv(g), x))
                        [by rewrite g-quotient-splits product-in-back]
                    }
                  @k-implies-g |
                    inH(op(inv(k), x)) -> inH(op(inv(g), x))
                    [by discharge given-in-k]
                  @conclusion-same-at-x |
                    inCoset(g, x) iff inCoset(k, x)
                    [using tautology criterion-g criterion-k g-implies-k k-implies-g]
                }
              @conclusion-backward |
                forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) iff inCoset(k, x)
                [by generalize generalize-x]
            }
          @backward |
            inH(op(inv(g), k)) -> (forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) iff inCoset(k, x))
            [by discharge given-quotient]
          @conclusion-iff |
            (forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) iff inCoset(k, x)) iff inH(op(inv(g), k))
            [by make_equivalence forward backward]
        }
      @discharge-k |
        forall k: G; (forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) iff inCoset(k, x)) iff inH(op(inv(g), k))
        [by generalize generalize-k]
    }
  @conclusion |
    forall g: G; forall k: G; (forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) iff inCoset(k, x)) iff inH(op(inv(g), k))
    [by generalize generalize-g]
qed
```

In all of his examples the cosets of a subgroup $H$ partition the larger group $G$. The
following theorem proclaims that this will always be the case.

## Theorem 6.4 — the cosets partition the group

**Theorem 6.4.** Let $H$ be a subgroup of a group $G$. Then the left cosets of $H$
in $G$ partition $G$. That is, the group $G$ is the disjoint union of the left
cosets of $H$ in $G$.

**Proof.** Let $g_1 H$ and $g_2 H$ be two cosets of $H$ in $G$. We must show
that either $g_1 H \cap g_2 H = \emptyset$ or $g_1 H = g_2 H$. Suppose that
$g_1 H \cap g_2 H \neq \emptyset$ and $a \in g_1 H \cap g_2 H$. Then by the
definition of a left coset, $a = g_1 h_1 = g_2 h_2$ for some elements $h_1$ and
$h_2$ in $H$. Hence, $g_1 = g_2 h_2 h_1^{-1}$ or $g_1 \in g_2 H$. By Lemma 6.3,
$g_1 H = g_2 H$. $\blacksquare$

A shared element forces the two cosets to agree everywhere; together with the fact
that every element lies in its own coset, this is the partition.

> NB: This theorem exists in std in `std/group/coset.b4m:453:cosetsAreEqualOrDisjoint`.

```2b4m
theorem cosetsAreEqualOrDisjoint: forall g: G; forall k: G; forall x: G;
  inCoset(g, x) -> inCoset(k, x) -> (forall y: G; inCoset(g, y) iff inCoset(k, y))
proof
  @generalize-g |
    fix g: G {
      @generalize-k |
        fix k: G {
          @generalize-x |
            fix x: G {
              @given-in-g |
                assume inCoset(g, x) {
                  @given-in-k |
                    assume inCoset(k, x) {
                      @in-g |
                        inCoset(g, x)
                        [by hypothesis given-in-g]
                      @in-k |
                        inCoset(k, x)
                        [by hypothesis given-in-k]
                      // g⁻¹x and k⁻¹x are both in H, so g⁻¹k = (g⁻¹x)(k⁻¹x)⁻¹ is too.
                      @criterion-g |
                        inCoset(g, x) iff inH(op(inv(g), x))
                        [using specialize inCosetIffQuotientIsInTheSubgroup(g, x)]
                      @criterion-k |
                        inCoset(k, x) iff inH(op(inv(k), x))
                        [using specialize inCosetIffQuotientIsInTheSubgroup(k, x)]
                      @g-quotient-in |
                        inH(op(inv(g), x))
                        [using tautology criterion-g in-g]
                      @k-quotient-in |
                        inH(op(inv(k), x))
                        [using tautology criterion-k in-k]
                      @k-quotient-inv-in |
                        inH(inv(op(inv(k), x)))
                        [using specialize subgroupClosedInverse(op(inv(k), x)) k-quotient-in]
                      @product-in |
                        inH(op(op(inv(g), x), inv(op(inv(k), x))))
                        [using specialize subgroupClosedOp(op(inv(g), x), inv(op(inv(k), x))) g-quotient-in k-quotient-inv-in]
                      @product-is-the-quotient |
                        op(op(inv(g), x), inv(op(inv(k), x))) = op(inv(g), k)
                        [using specialize quotientsCombine(g, k, x)]
                      @quotient-in |
                        inH(op(inv(g), k))
                        [by rewrite product-is-the-quotient product-in]
                      @coincide |
                        (forall y: G; inCoset(g, y) iff inCoset(k, y)) iff inH(op(inv(g), k))
                        [using specialize cosetsCoincideIffQuotientIsInTheSubgroup(g, k)]
                      @conclusion-same |
                        forall y: G; inCoset(g, y) iff inCoset(k, y)
                        [using tautology coincide quotient-in]
                    }
                  @in-k-implies |
                    inCoset(k, x) -> (forall y: G; inCoset(g, y) iff inCoset(k, y))
                    [by discharge given-in-k]
                }
              @in-g-implies |
                inCoset(g, x) -> inCoset(k, x) -> (forall y: G; inCoset(g, y) iff inCoset(k, y))
                [by discharge given-in-g]
            }
          @discharge-x |
            forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) -> inCoset(k, x) -> (forall y: G; inCoset(g, y) iff inCoset(k, y))
            [by generalize generalize-x]
        }
      @discharge-k |
        forall k: G; forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) -> inCoset(k, x) -> (forall y: G; inCoset(g, y) iff inCoset(k, y))
        [by generalize generalize-k]
    }
  @conclusion |
    forall g: G; forall k: G; forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) -> inCoset(k, x) -> (forall y: G; inCoset(g, y) iff inCoset(k, y))
    [by generalize generalize-g]
qed
```

There is nothing special in this theorem about left cosets. Right cosets also
partition $G$; the proof of this fact is exactly the same as the proof for left
cosets except that all group multiplications are done on the opposite side of $H$.

## The index

**Definition.** Let $G$ be a group and $H$ be a subgroup of $G$. Define the
**index** of $H$ in $G$ to be the number of left cosets of $H$ in $G$. We will
denote the index by $[G:H]$.

**Theorem 6.7.** Let $H$ be a subgroup of a group $G$. The number of left cosets of
$H$ in $G$ is the same as the number of right cosets of $H$ in $G$.

**Proof.** Let $\mathcal{L}_H$ and $\mathcal{R}_H$ denote the set of left and right
cosets of $H$ in $G$, respectively. If we can define a bijective map
$\phi : \mathcal{L}_H \rightarrow \mathcal{R}_H$, then the theorem will be proved. If
$gH \in \mathcal{L}_H$, let $\phi(gH) = Hg^{-1}$. By Lemma 6.3, the map $\phi$ is
well-defined; that is, if $g_1H = g_2H$, then $Hg_1^{-1} = Hg_2^{-1}$. To show that
$\phi$ is one-to-one, suppose that
$Hg_1^{-1} = \phi(g_1H) = \phi(g_2H) = Hg_2^{-1}$. Again by Lemma 6.3,
$g_1H = g_2H$. The map $\phi$ is onto since $\phi(g^{-1}H) = Hg$. $\blacksquare$

Both directions of $\phi$ appeal to the same fact, the right-coset analogue of Lemma
6.3: two right cosets coincide exactly when $kg^{-1}$ lies in $H$. Since $k$ lies in its
own right coset, agreement puts $k$ in $Hg$, so $k = hg$ for some $h \in H$, and
$kg^{-1} = (hg)g^{-1} = h$.

```2b4m
// Hg = Hk forces k·g⁻¹ ∈ H — the mirror of Lemma 6.3, which is what phi's
// well-definedness and injectivity both rest on.
define inRightCoset(g, x) = exists h: G; inH(h) and x = op(h, g)

theorem rightCosetsCoincideIffProductIsInTheSubgroup: forall g: G; forall k: G;
  (forall x: G; inRightCoset(g, x) iff inRightCoset(k, x)) -> inH(op(k, inv(g)))
proof
  @generalize-g |
    fix g: G {
      @generalize-k |
        fix k: G {
          @given-the-right-cosets-agree |
            assume forall x: G; inRightCoset(g, x) iff inRightCoset(k, x) {
              @the-cosets-agree |
                forall x: G; inRightCoset(g, x) iff inRightCoset(k, x)
                [by hypothesis given-the-right-cosets-agree]
              @identity-in-h |
                inH(e)
                [by cite subgroupHasIdentity]
              @the-identity-absorbs-k |
                op(e, k) = k
                [using specialize grpIdentityLeft(k)]
              @k-is-the-identity-times-k |
                k = op(e, k)
                [by symmetry the-identity-absorbs-k]
              @k-witnesses |
                inH(e) and k = op(e, k)
                [by both identity-in-h k-is-the-identity-times-k]
              @k-is-in-its-own-right-coset |
                inRightCoset(k, k)
                [by witness(e) k-witnesses]
              @the-cosets-agree-at-k |
                inRightCoset(g, k) iff inRightCoset(k, k)
                [using specialize the-cosets-agree(k)]
              @k-is-in-gs-right-coset |
                inRightCoset(g, k)
                [using tautology the-cosets-agree-at-k k-is-in-its-own-right-coset]
              @with-the-factor |
                unpack h: G from k-is-in-gs-right-coset {
                  @the-factor |
                    inH(h) and k = op(h, g)
                    [by hypothesis with-the-factor]
                  @h-is-in-h |
                    inH(h)
                    [by and_lhs the-factor]
                  @k-is-h-times-g |
                    k = op(h, g)
                    [by and_rhs the-factor]
                  // (hg)g⁻¹ = h(gg⁻¹) = h·e = h
                  @regroup |
                    op(op(h, g), inv(g)) = op(h, op(g, inv(g)))
                    [using assoc(grpAssoc)]
                  @g-kills-its-inverse |
                    op(g, inv(g)) = e
                    [using specialize grpInverseRight(g)]
                  @h-absorbs-the-identity |
                    op(h, e) = h
                    [using specialize grpIdentityRight(h)]
                  @cancel-g |
                    op(op(h, g), inv(g)) = h
                    [using chain regroup g-kills-its-inverse h-absorbs-the-identity]
                  @cancel-g-gives-h |
                    inH(op(op(h, g), inv(g)))
                    [by rewrite cancel-g h-is-in-h]
                  @conclusion-the-product-is-in-h |
                    inH(op(k, inv(g)))
                    [by rewrite k-is-h-times-g cancel-g-gives-h]
                }
              @conclusion-after-the-factor |
                inH(op(k, inv(g)))
                [by unpacked with-the-factor]
            }
          @conclusion-at-g-k |
            (forall x: G; inRightCoset(g, x) iff inRightCoset(k, x)) -> inH(op(k, inv(g)))
            [by discharge given-the-right-cosets-agree]
        }
      @discharge-k |
        forall k: G;
          (forall x: G; inRightCoset(g, x) iff inRightCoset(k, x)) -> inH(op(k, inv(g)))
        [by generalize generalize-k]
    }
  @conclusion |
    forall g: G; forall k: G;
      (forall x: G; inRightCoset(g, x) iff inRightCoset(k, x)) -> inH(op(k, inv(g)))
    [by generalize generalize-g]
qed
```

> *The bijection $\phi$ itself cannot be named: $\mathcal{L}_H$ and $\mathcal{R}_H$ are
> sets of cosets, and a coset here is a membership predicate rather than an element of a
> sort, so there is nothing for $\phi$ to be a map between. The criterion above is the
> content his proof appeals to in both directions.*

That every coset has the *same* size is translation by $g$ mapping $H$ onto $gH$
injectively, so a count of $H$ of size $p$ yields a count of $gH$ of size $p$. The index
itself is taken up in §6.2.

> NB: This theorem exists in std in `std/group/coset.b4m:641:everyCosetIsListedByTheSubgroupsLength`.

```2b4m
theorem everyCosetIsListedByTheSubgroupsLength: forall g: G; forall v: Seq; forall p: Nat;
  listsTheSubgroup(v, p) -> exists u: Seq; listsTheCoset(g, u, p)
proof
  @generalize-g |
    fix g: G {
      @generalize-v |
        fix v: Seq {
          @generalize-p |
            fix p: Nat {
              @given-lists |
                assume listsTheSubgroup(v, p) {
                  @lists |
                    listsTheSubgroup(v, p)
                    [by hypothesis given-lists]
                  @entries-in |
                    forall i: Nat; less_than(i, p) -> inH(at(v, i))
                    [using tautology lists]
                  @injective |
                    forall i: Nat; forall j: Nat; less_than(i, p) -> less_than(j, p) -> at(v, i) = at(v, j) -> i = j
                    [using tautology lists]
                  @covers |
                    forall h: G; inH(h) -> exists i: Nat; less_than(i, p) and at(v, i) = h
                    [using tautology lists]
                  @translate-exists |
                    exists u: Seq; forall j: Nat; less_than(j, p) -> at(u, j) = op(g, at(v, j))
                    [using specialize group_sequence.translatedSequenceExists(g, v, p)]
                  @with-translate |
                    unpack u: Seq from translate-exists {
                      @translate |
                        forall j: Nat; less_than(j, p) -> at(u, j) = op(g, at(v, j))
                        [by hypothesis with-translate]
                      // every entry of u lies in gH
                      @entries-in-coset |
                        forall i: Nat; less_than(i, p) -> inCoset(g, at(u, i))
                        [by generalize entry-in-coset]
                      @entry-in-coset |
                        fix i: Nat {
                          @given-below |
                            assume less_than(i, p) {
                              @below |
                                less_than(i, p)
                                [by hypothesis given-below]
                              @entry-is-translated |
                                at(u, i) = op(g, at(v, i))
                                [using specialize translate(i) below]
                              @subgroup-entry |
                                inH(at(v, i))
                                [using specialize entries-in(i) below]
                              @translated-in-coset |
                                inCoset(g, op(g, at(v, i)))
                                [using specialize translationLandsInTheCoset(g, at(v, i)) subgroup-entry]
                              @conclusion-entry-in |
                                inCoset(g, at(u, i))
                                [by rewrite entry-is-translated translated-in-coset]
                            }
                          @below-implies |
                            less_than(i, p) -> inCoset(g, at(u, i))
                            [by discharge given-below]
                        }
                      // u is injective: g·v_i = g·v_j cancels to v_i = v_j
                      @translate-injective |
                        forall i: Nat; forall j: Nat; less_than(i, p) -> less_than(j, p) -> at(u, i) = at(u, j) -> i = j
                        [by generalize injective-outer]
                      @injective-outer |
                        fix i: Nat {
                          @injective-inner |
                            fix j: Nat {
                              @given-i-below |
                                assume less_than(i, p) {
                                  @given-j-below |
                                    assume less_than(j, p) {
                                      @given-entries-equal |
                                        assume at(u, i) = at(u, j) {
                                          @i-below |
                                            less_than(i, p)
                                            [by hypothesis given-i-below]
                                          @j-below |
                                            less_than(j, p)
                                            [by hypothesis given-j-below]
                                          @entries-equal |
                                            at(u, i) = at(u, j)
                                            [by hypothesis given-entries-equal]
                                          @u-at-i |
                                            at(u, i) = op(g, at(v, i))
                                            [using specialize translate(i) i-below]
                                          @u-at-j |
                                            at(u, j) = op(g, at(v, j))
                                            [using specialize translate(j) j-below]
                                          @translates-equal |
                                            op(g, at(v, i)) = op(g, at(v, j))
                                            [using chain u-at-i-flipped entries-equal u-at-j]
                                          @u-at-i-flipped |
                                            op(g, at(v, i)) = at(u, i)
                                            [by symmetry u-at-i]
                                          @originals-equal |
                                            at(v, i) = at(v, j)
                                            [using specialize translationIsInjective(g, at(v, i), at(v, j)) translates-equal]
                                          @conclusion-indices-equal |
                                            i = j
                                            [using specialize injective(i, j) i-below j-below originals-equal]
                                        }
                                      @equal-implies |
                                        at(u, i) = at(u, j) -> i = j
                                        [by discharge given-entries-equal]
                                    }
                                  @j-below-implies |
                                    less_than(j, p) -> at(u, i) = at(u, j) -> i = j
                                    [by discharge given-j-below]
                                }
                              @i-below-implies |
                                less_than(i, p) -> less_than(j, p) -> at(u, i) = at(u, j) -> i = j
                                [by discharge given-i-below]
                            }
                          @discharge-inner |
                            forall j: Nat; less_than(i, p) -> less_than(j, p) -> at(u, i) = at(u, j) -> i = j
                            [by generalize injective-inner]
                        }
                      // u covers gH: x in gH is g·h, h is v_i, so x is u_i
                      @covers-the-coset |
                        forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) -> exists i: Nat; less_than(i, p) and at(u, i) = x
                        [by generalize covers-x]
                      @covers-x |
                        fix x: G {
                          @given-in-coset |
                            assume inCoset(g, x) {
                              @in-coset |
                                inCoset(g, x)
                                [by hypothesis given-in-coset]
                              @h-exists |
                                exists h: G; inH(h) and x = op(g, h)
                                [using specialize everyCosetMemberIsATranslation(g, x) in-coset]
                              @with-h |
                                unpack h: G from h-exists {
                                  @h-facts |
                                    inH(h) and x = op(g, h)
                                    [by hypothesis with-h]
                                  @h-in |
                                    inH(h)
                                    [by and_lhs h-facts]
                                  @x-is-translated |
                                    x = op(g, h)
                                    [by and_rhs h-facts]
                                  @index-exists |
                                    exists i: Nat; less_than(i, p) and at(v, i) = h
                                    [using specialize covers(h) h-in]
                                  @with-index |
                                    unpack i: Nat from index-exists {
                                      @index-facts |
                                        less_than(i, p) and at(v, i) = h
                                        [by hypothesis with-index]
                                      @i-below |
                                        less_than(i, p)
                                        [by and_lhs index-facts]
                                      @v-at-i-is-h |
                                        at(v, i) = h
                                        [by and_rhs index-facts]
                                      @u-at-i |
                                        at(u, i) = op(g, at(v, i))
                                        [using specialize translate(i) i-below]
                                      @u-at-i-is-g-h |
                                        at(u, i) = op(g, h)
                                        [by rewrite v-at-i-is-h u-at-i]
                                      @x-is-u-at-i |
                                        op(g, h) = x
                                        [by symmetry x-is-translated]
                                      @u-at-i-is-x |
                                        at(u, i) = x
                                        [using chain u-at-i-is-g-h x-is-u-at-i]
                                      @witnessed |
                                        less_than(i, p) and at(u, i) = x
                                        [by both i-below u-at-i-is-x]
                                      @conclusion-index-inner |
                                        exists i2: Nat; less_than(i2, p) and at(u, i2) = x
                                        [by witness(i) witnessed]
                                    }
                                  @conclusion-index-mid |
                                    exists i: Nat; less_than(i, p) and at(u, i) = x
                                    [by unpacked with-index]
                                }
                              @conclusion-covers-x |
                                exists i: Nat; less_than(i, p) and at(u, i) = x
                                [by unpacked with-h]
                            }
                          @in-coset-implies |
                            inCoset(g, x) -> exists i: Nat; less_than(i, p) and at(u, i) = x
                            [by discharge given-in-coset]
                        }
                      @all-three |
                        listsTheCoset(g, u, p)
                        [using tautology entries-in-coset translate-injective covers-the-coset]
                      @conclusion-coset-listed-inner |
                        exists u2: Seq; listsTheCoset(g, u2, p)
                        [by witness(u) all-three]
                    }
                  @conclusion-coset-listed |
                    exists u: Seq; listsTheCoset(g, u, p)
                    [by unpacked with-translate]
                }
              @lists-implies |
                listsTheSubgroup(v, p) -> exists u: Seq; listsTheCoset(g, u, p)
                [by discharge given-lists]
            }
          @discharge-p |
            forall p: Nat; listsTheSubgroup(v, p) -> exists u: Seq; listsTheCoset(g, u, p)
            [by generalize generalize-p]
        }
      @discharge-v |
        forall v: Seq; forall p: Nat; listsTheSubgroup(v, p) -> exists u: Seq; listsTheCoset(g, u, p)
        [by generalize generalize-v]
    }
  @conclusion |
    forall g: G; forall v: Seq; forall p: Nat; listsTheSubgroup(v, p) -> exists u: Seq; listsTheCoset(g, u, p)
    [by generalize generalize-g]
qed
```

The two halves of that bijection are available separately — translation lands in the
coset and is injective, and every coset member is a translation.

> NB: This theorem exists in std in `std/group/coset.b4m:596:translationIsInjective`.

```2b4m
theorem translationLandsInTheCoset: forall g: G; forall h: G; inH(h) -> inCoset(g, op(g, h))
proof
  @generalize-g |
    fix g: G {
      @generalize-h |
        fix h: G {
          @given-in |
            assume inH(h) {
              @h-in |
                inH(h)
                [by hypothesis given-in]
              @reflexive |
                op(g, h) = op(g, h)
                [by reflexivity]
              @witnessed |
                inH(h) and op(g, h) = op(g, h)
                [by both h-in reflexive]
              @conclusion-in-coset |
                inCoset(g, op(g, h))
                [by witness(h) witnessed]
            }
          @in-implies |
            inH(h) -> inCoset(g, op(g, h))
            [by discharge given-in]
        }
      @discharge-h |
        forall h: G; inH(h) -> inCoset(g, op(g, h))
        [by generalize generalize-h]
    }
  @conclusion |
    forall g: G; forall h: G; inH(h) -> inCoset(g, op(g, h))
    [by generalize generalize-g]
qed
theorem translationIsInjective: forall g: G; forall h: G; forall k: G;
  op(g, h) = op(g, k) -> h = k
proof
  @generalize-g |
    fix g: G {
      @generalize-h |
        fix h: G {
          @generalize-k |
            fix k: G {
              @given-equal |
                assume op(g, h) = op(g, k) {
                  @equal |
                    op(g, h) = op(g, k)
                    [by hypothesis given-equal]
                  @conclusion-cancelled |
                    h = k
                    [using specialize cancelLeft(g, h, k) equal]
                }
              @equal-implies |
                op(g, h) = op(g, k) -> h = k
                [by discharge given-equal]
            }
          @discharge-k |
            forall k: G; op(g, h) = op(g, k) -> h = k
            [by generalize generalize-k]
        }
      @discharge-h |
        forall h: G; forall k: G; op(g, h) = op(g, k) -> h = k
        [by generalize generalize-h]
    }
  @conclusion |
    forall g: G; forall h: G; forall k: G; op(g, h) = op(g, k) -> h = k
    [by generalize generalize-g]
qed
theorem everyCosetMemberIsATranslation: forall g: G; forall x: G;
  inCoset(g, x) -> (exists h: G; inH(h) and x = op(g, h))
proof
  @generalize-g |
    fix g: G {
      @generalize-x |
        fix x: G {
          @given-in-coset |
            assume inCoset(g, x) {
              @in-coset |
                inCoset(g, x)
                [by hypothesis given-in-coset]
              @conclusion-unpacked |
                exists h: G; inH(h) and x = op(g, h)
                [using tautology in-coset]
            }
          @in-coset-implies |
            inCoset(g, x) -> (exists h: G; inH(h) and x = op(g, h))
            [by discharge given-in-coset]
        }
      @discharge-x |
        forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) -> (exists h: G; inH(h) and x = op(g, h))
        [by generalize generalize-x]
    }
  @conclusion |
    forall g: G; forall x: G; inCoset(g, x) -> (exists h: G; inH(h) and x = op(g, h))
    [by generalize generalize-g]
qed
```
