# The Method of Repeated Squares (Exercises)

Exercises for Judson **§4.3 "The Method of Repeated Squares"** (source:
`vendor/aata/src/cyclic.xml`; © 1997–2026 Judson, GFDL 1.3), following
`.claude/rules/aata-guide.md`.

**22. Compute** $292^{3171} \pmod{582}$, $2557^{341} \pmod{5681}$, $2071^{9521}
\pmod{4724}$, $971^{321} \pmod{765}$. **Skipped** — these are brute modular-
exponentiation computations, the numeric drill for the repeated-squares algorithm
of §4.3. As noted there, encoding even a statement about the algorithm needs a
concrete modular-arithmetic sort plus binary-expansion machinery; the individual
numeric answers are decidable evaluation, not proof content, so they are skipped
(consistent with the corpus's treatment of brute numeric exercises).
